## The project

Halo 5 Reforged is a community effort to bring the complete **Halo 5: Guardians**
experience to Windows PC: Campaign, co-op, multiplayer, Warzone, Forge, and Spartan
customization. LAN and mod support are longer-term goals.

Built around the PC foundation of **Halo 5: Forge**, Reforged brings together
project-authored runtime code, content tools, a desktop launcher, community
services, and dedicated servers. Original game files are supplied by each user
for local processing.

> **In active development.** There is no public playable release or announced
> release date. The milestones below describe development builds. See the
> [release FAQ](https://h5reforged.dev/faq.html#release) for availability and testing updates.

## Where things stand

Public progress snapshot: **October 2026**. The [roadmap](https://h5reforged.dev/roadmap.html)
tracks working features, ongoing work, and plans.

| Area | Progress in development builds |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | All 15 missions load into gameplay. Osiris has been completed on all four difficulties; completion checks for the remaining missions continue. |
| **Co-op** | Two-player online Campaign has been tested on a separate dedicated server. Checkpoint reliability, sustained play, and mission transitions need more work; four-player support is planned. |
| **Multiplayer** | Custom Games, saved custom modes, friends, and fireteam invitations are working. Bots are experimental; Arena playlists and matchmaking are planned. |
| **Warzone** | All eight Firefight maps pass initial loading, first-round, and return-to-menu checks. Full five-round coverage, in-match REQ use, and reliable results remain in progress. |
| **Forge** | The editor loads, and maps can be saved and reopened with placed objects. The full saved-map-to-Custom-Games flow is still being validated. |
| **Spartan & REQ** | Individual armor customization and earned unlocks persist. REQ packs can be bought with in-game points and opened; cards can be collected and sold. Names and thumbnails still need work. |
| **PC experience** | A development launcher supports resumable installs and updates. HDR and experimental view and HUD controls are available in development builds. |

See the [devlog](https://h5reforged.dev/devlog.html) for dated milestones and
[screenshots](https://h5reforged.dev/#screenshots) captured in Reforged.

## What makes up Reforged

This organization is home to the wider project:

| Part | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Game & launcher** | The PC runtime, menus, local content preparation, installation, and updates. |
| **Community services** | Reforged accounts, friends, fireteams, progression, customization, and REQ inventory. |
| **Dedicated servers** | Hosting for Reforged game sessions, including Campaign co-op and Custom Games. |
| **Creator tools** | Content conversion and variant editing tools that support development and experimentation. |
| **Documentation** | Setup guides, architecture, compatibility findings, and records of verified progress. |
| **Website & community** | Public updates, the roadmap, screenshots, and community creations. |

## Project policy & credits

Reforged is free and non-commercial. We do not sell access, offer paid early
access, or accept donations. Users must supply original game files from copies
they are lawfully entitled to access; Microsoft-owned game files are not included
in project releases. See the [project policy](https://h5reforged.dev/#legal).

Halo 5 Reforged is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with and not endorsed
by Microsoft, Xbox, or Halo Studios. Halo and its game content belong to their
respective rights holders.

Thanks to the contributors and the wider Halo research and tool-making community
whose work supports Reforged. Meet them on the [credits page](https://h5reforged.dev/credits.html).
